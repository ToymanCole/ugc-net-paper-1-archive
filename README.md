# UGC NET Paper 1 Archive — verification manifest

Checked on 1 November 2026.

## What is included

`index.html` is a responsive, client-side searchable archive covering the six latest completed cycles represented in the NTA record:

1. December 2025
2. June 2025
3. December 2024 (exam held in January 2025)
4. June 2024, re-conducted in August–September 2024
5. December 2023
6. June 2023

The `pdfs/` folder contains five public, date-and-shift-matched convenience paper/key pairs (morning shift): 31 Dec 2025, 25 Jun 2025, 21 Aug 2024, 6 Dec 2023 and 13 Jun 2023. Each pair was confirmed from the same provider index entry. They are attributed third-party copies, not official NTA publications.

## Provenance

* **Official NTA:** https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in/archive/ — examination notices and key-related records. The archive establishes that June 2026 had application notices but no completed-exam record at the check date.
* **Official June 2025 key collection:** https://ugcnet.nta.ac.in/AnswerKey_june2025.html — final keys are provided by subject; Paper 1 appears alongside Paper 2 in subject papers.
* **Third-party PDFs/index:** https://humanperitus.in/ugc-net-paper-1-previous-papers/ — date/shift-labelled Paper 1 copies and keys. The archive links its full listing, rather than asserting ownership of all copies.
* **December 2024 gap:** https://testbook.com/ugc-net/paper-1-previous-year-papers — shifts are indexed, but a direct downloadable Paper 1 PDF plus a matching final key was not independently verified and is deliberately marked missing.

## Important limitations

NTA’s public archive does not operate as a permanent, universal Paper 1 PDF repository; candidate papers and provisional keys are ordinarily shown during a logged-in challenge window. “Official final answer key” does not mean a single standalone Paper 1 key: NTA final keys are subject-specific. The archive never presents a third-party answer key as an NTA final key.

The cancelled 18 June 2024 paper is excluded from the re-conducted June 2024 practice set.
